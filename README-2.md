# AIOps: Listen

An interactive listening app for the audio deep dive **How AIOps Fixes IT Alert Storms**, based on the *AIOps Manual*, a 15-chapter reference on AI for IT operations.

The 57-minute episode starts with an on-call engineer drowning in hundreds of alerts on a Friday night, then works through the whole AIOps stack: telemetry pipelines, anomaly detection, root cause analysis, alert correlation, self-healing, MLOps, security, LLM agents, governance and the future of human-AI teaming.

**▶ Open it: https://kzaza17.github.io/AIOps/**

## Features

- **Synced pages:** 85 pages of the manual appear on screen as the presenters reach them
- **24 chapters:** jump straight to any topic, from the base rate problem to MCP and digital twins
- **Reading mode:** the text of each page discussed; tap a paragraph to play the part of the audio that covers it
- **Transcript mode:** captions light up word by word; tap any line to jump there
- **Full-screen player:** chapter markers, 15-second skips, speeds from 0.75× to 2×
- **Remembers where you were:** your position and saved chapters are kept in your browser
- **Works on phones:** Android and iOS, no app or install needed

## Files

- `index.html`: the whole app in one file, with the audio, pages and transcript built in, so it also works offline
- `AIOps_captions.en.srt`: English captions for the episode
